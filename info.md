# Grandstream GDMS Integration for Home Assistant

Monitor and manage your **Grandstream Device Management System (GDMS)** cloud fleet (IP phones, ATAs, Wi-Fi APs, routers, and door systems) directly inside Home Assistant.

### Features
- **Automatic Fleet Discovery:** Imports all devices across your GDMS organizations and sites.
- **Live Status & SIP Monitoring:** Connectivity binary sensors, SIP account registration status, local/public IPs, and firmware versions.
- **Remote Device Control:** One-click **Reboot** and **Push Configuration** buttons, plus custom task automation services.
- **Multi-Region Support:** Native support for Americas/World (`www.gdms.cloud`), Europe (`eu.gdms.cloud`), and Oceania (`oc.gdms.cloud`).
